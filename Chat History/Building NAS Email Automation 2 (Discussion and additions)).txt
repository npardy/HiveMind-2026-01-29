Two good questions. Let me be honest about both.

Is there a simpler built-in approach?

Not really. Here's what exists:

Option	Why it doesn't fit
Claude.ai integrations (Google Drive, etc.)	No QBO, no custom file system, can't run autonomously
MCP (Model Context Protocol)	Same pattern we built - just a different way to define tools. Not simpler, just standardized
Zapier / Make / Power Automate	Rule-based, not reasoning. "If subject contains X, do Y." Can't handle judgment calls
Microsoft Copilot	Locked to Microsoft ecosystem, can't touch QBO or your NAS the way you need
Off-the-shelf email AI tools	Generic. Don't know surveying, can't create QBO projects, can't structure job folders your way
What you built is genuinely the right approach for what you want. The value is that Claude reasons about each email rather than matching patterns. That requires the tool-based architecture.

The only simplification might be switching the tool definitions to MCP format someday, but that's just a protocol change - same logic underneath.

Should Claude have context from previous emails?

Right now each email is processed independently. Claude doesn't know "I handled something from Gary about this same property yesterday" unless it's in the same thread.

What context could help:

"I quoted $1,200 for a similar job in Paradise last week" → Pricing consistency
"This is the 12th job from Van Driel Law this quarter" → Relationship awareness
"Gary always asks for invoices to be resent" → Anticipate needs
"I already dealt with this thread yesterday" → Avoid duplicate work
"Last time I flagged something like this, Nick changed my response" → Learn preferences
How it could work:

Simple version: A rolling log of recent actions

Last 50 emails:
- gnolan@rvdslaw.ca | Job request | Created 26-011, sent invoice | Jan 17
- jsmith@gmail.com | Quote request | Flagged - non-metro | Jan 17
- propertyonline@novascotia.ca | System notice | Flagged | Jan 16
...
Claude sees this at the start of each processing run. Adds context without complexity.

Fancier version: Per-contact memory, pattern learning, preference tracking. But that's more infrastructure.

This is solvable but the tricky part is capturing the info at the right moment.

The problem:

Cheque arrives with invoice stub attached
You deposit it (app or lump sum at bank)
Later, bank feed shows money came in
Matching deposit to invoice = detective work, especially lump sums
The fix - capture at time of receipt:

Cheque arrives in mail
    ↓
Take photo of cheque + invoice stub
    ↓
Photo goes to Claude somehow (email? synced folder? text?)
    ↓
Claude reads: Amount, payer, invoice number/address
    ↓
Claude matches to open invoice in QBO
    ↓
Records payment in QBO (not deposited yet)
    ↓
You deposit however you want
    ↓
Bank feed comes in, reconciliation is just confirming what's already recorded
The question is: how does the photo get to Claude?

Options:

Method	Pros	Cons
Email photo to dedicated address	Simple, works from any phone	Extra step
Synced folder (OneDrive/Dropbox)	Drop photo, auto-processes	Need to set up sync
Text/WhatsApp to Vapi number	Natural, already have phone out	Need Vapi image handling
Simple mobile app	Could be streamlined	Need to build it
For lump sum deposits, you'd photograph each cheque before going to the bank. Claude records each payment individually in QBO. Bank shows one deposit, but QBO already knows it's made up of 5 payments to 5 invoices.
